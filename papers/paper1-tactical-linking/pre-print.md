For a paper on a **Target-Centric Knowledge Graph Architecture / Decision Support System for Law Enforcement**, the ideal repository depends on whether your primary target is Computer Science indexing, Engineering visibility, or Multidisciplinary open science.

---

### **Top Recommended Pre-Print Repositories**

#### **1. arXiv (Top Recommendation for CS & Information Systems)**

* **Best Category:** `cs.AI` (Artificial Intelligence), `cs.SE` (Software Engineering), or `cs.DB` (Databases).
* **Why it fits:** It is the undisputed global gold standard for computer science, software architecture, and AI research. Indexing on arXiv guarantees immediate inclusion in **Google Scholar, DBLP, and Semantic Scholar** within days.
* **Key Considerations:** Requires an "endorsement" from an existing arXiv user if you haven't posted in that specific sub-category before. It also has strict LaTeX/PDF compilation checks.

#### **2. TechRxiv (IEEE's Pre-Print Server)**

* **Best Focus:** Information technology, system architectures, and applied computer engineering.
* **Why it fits:** Operated by the **IEEE**, TechRxiv is ideal if you plan to eventually submit the peer-reviewed version to an IEEE journal (e.g., *IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering* or *IEEE Access*).
* **Key Considerations:** Extremely straightforward upload process, accepts direct PDF uploads, assigns a DOI quickly, and has no endorsement hurdle.

#### **3. Research Square / SSRN**

* **Best Focus:** Multidisciplinary systems, Decision Support Systems (DSS), Social Computing, and Applied Policy/Public Systems.
* **Why it fits:** Excellent if your paper leans heavily toward the operational, organizational, and decision-making framework (ILP / Clark's target-centric model) rather than pure code/data structures.
* **Key Considerations:** Assigns DOIs rapidly and tracks download metrics/altmetrics thoroughly.

### **Strategic Recommendation for Your Project**

1. **If you want maximum academic weight in Computer Science & Information Systems:**
Go with **arXiv** (`cs.AI` or `cs.SE`). It carries the highest prestige among technical reviewers and guarantees your architecture is cited as prior art globally.
